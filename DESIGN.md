# Chroniques Ligériennes — Design Direction

## Brand idea

Treat the site as a **civic field journal**: an editorial record of Montaine's experience inside regional event production. It should feel personal and curious while carrying the confidence and clarity of a public institution.

Preserve these recognizable elements:

- the three-part ISEFAC / Région / Chroniques masthead;
- Région Pays de la Loire blue;
- documentary photographs from the internship;
- Montaine's warm first-person voice;
- the distinction between published chronicles and behind-the-scenes work.

## Visual system

- **Primary:** `#10069f`; **deep blue:** `#090557`; **ink:** `#171721`.
- **Paper:** `#f8f6f0`; **deep paper:** `#ebe6da`; **accent:** `#cfe83d`.
- Use warm paper surfaces instead of pure grey or generic white cards.
- Use an editorial serif for display headings and a restrained sans serif for interface text.
- Use large, tightly set headlines, compact uppercase labels, fine rules, and generous negative space.
- Use rounded corners selectively for framed stories, media, and cards—not for every element.

## Composition

- Prefer asymmetric editorial layouts and strong image crops.
- Keep long-form article text near 740px wide for comfortable reading.
- Let the homepage hero and article titles carry the strongest visual impact.
- Use the green found in the hero image as a precise accent, not as a second dominant palette.

## Interaction

- Keep motion quiet: image scale on hover, subtle entrance reveals, and a reading-progress line on articles.
- Preserve clear active navigation, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support.
- Never introduce horizontal scrolling at phone widths.

## Avoid

- generic startup gradients, glass cards, or unrelated component-library styling;
- default-looking centered stacks and evenly weighted sections;
- replacing the institutional blue or hiding the partner logos;
- decorative motion that competes with reading;
- changing the personal French copy into corporate marketing language.
