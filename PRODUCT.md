# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students, educators, regional partners, colleagues, and future employers who want to understand Montaine Poincloux's two-month internship through the work produced, the people involved, and the lessons learned.

## Product Purpose

Chroniques Ligériennes is a personal internship journal and documentary portfolio. It makes a regional event-production placement tangible through first-person French writing, real project artifacts, partner identities, and photography from the field.

## Brand Personality

Candid, grounded, curious. The experience should feel considered and confident while retaining the warmth and slight irregularity of a real journal written by one person.

## Anti-references

Generic startup landing pages, AI-purple gradients, interchangeable card grids, anonymous corporate portfolios, decorative glass effects, overproduced luxury-editorial styling, and motion that competes with the writing or documentary imagery.

## Design Principles

1. Let evidence lead: real photography, documents, logos, and first-person observations carry the story.
2. Make the regional identity unmistakable: build from the established blue palette and partner marks without turning the site into an institutional template.
3. Preserve the author's voice: improve legibility and structure without flattening the French editorial tone or personal details.
4. Pace the journal deliberately: use hierarchy, whitespace, and composition to guide readers through long-form stories.
5. Make refinement inclusive: every visual and interaction decision must remain clear with keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and small screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide semantic landmarks and headings, descriptive alternative text, visible focus states, 44px touch targets, sufficient contrast, predictable navigation, a skip link, robust text zoom, and reduced-motion behavior.
